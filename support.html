Support

iCloud Music Player

Where does my music go?

Nowhere. Spine plays the folder in place. It never renames, moves, uploads or edits anything in it.

To play a song, Spine fetches a copy into its own storage. That copy is what lets playback start before an album has finished downloading. It is also the only thing the budget governs, and the only thing "Remove all downloads", or deleting the app, takes away.

How do I start?

  • Put your music in a folder in iCloud Drive. Artist and album folders work. So do loose files.
  • Open Spine and choose that folder once. It remembers.
  • No library yet? "Try a sample library" writes two small albums into the app's folder.

Which files can it play?

FLAC, WAV, AIFF, ALAC, M4A, MP3 and AAC, in any subfolder. Anything else is listed as unplayable instead of skipped silently. A folder that shows nothing is telling you the truth.

Why is it downloading?

A file you never opened in iCloud Drive is a placeholder: a name and a size, no audio. Spine fetches a track when you press play, so the next play is instant. It also fetches the next few songs ahead when your settings allow it.

What does the budget do?

It caps what Spine keeps on the phone. Past it, the least recently played files are evicted — the originals stay in iCloud Drive and return when you play them again. The song you are listening to, and the ones queued behind it, are never evicted.

Can I free the space now?

Settings → "Remove all downloads". Every copy Spine fetched leaves the phone. Your folder is untouched.

Does it work without a signal?

For anything already downloaded, yes. For anything still in iCloud Drive, no — the file has to arrive first. If you are about to be offline, download the albums you want while you have a connection.

What is passenger control?

A nearby phone can change what is playing. It runs directly between the two, encrypted, with no server and no account. You approve a guest once, and can forget it later.

How do I report a problem?

Open an issue on the public tracker. Useful details: the iPhone model, the iOS version, what you expected, what happened, and — if it is about a file — the format and roughly how large it is. Never attach music you do not own the rights to.

Where is the beta?

In TestFlight. The public link and the App Store release are announced here first.
