iCloud Music Player

Your music, played from iCloud Drive.

Point Spine at the folder you already keep there. Songs download when you play them, and stay only while your budget allows.

01 — The budget

Built for a phone that is already full.

Set a ceiling. Oldest plays leave first. The song playing, and the few behind it, never leave.

02 — The folder

Point at it once.

Spine reads names, sizes and tags. No audio. A song is fetched when you press play.

03 — The passenger

Let the back seat pick the next song.

A passenger opens Spine. You approve them once. They get the queue and the transport, phone to phone, encrypted.
