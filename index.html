iCloud Music Player

Your music, played from iCloud Drive.

Spine points at the folder you already keep there. It reads what is in it, fetches a song when you press play, and keeps no more on the phone than the budget you set.

01 — The budget

A phone that is already full. That is the case this was built for.

You set a ceiling in Settings and Spine stays under it. The songs you played longest ago are evicted first; the one in your ears, and the few queued behind it, never are. Your library is untouched in iCloud Drive the whole time.

02 — The folder

Point at it once. Spine keeps up without downloading it.

It reads the folder periodically — names, sizes and tags only — so the shelf is there before a single byte of audio. A song is fetched when you press play, not before, and the copy it keeps is what the budget governs.

03 — The passenger

Let the back seat pick the next song.

A passenger opens Spine on their phone, your phone approves it once, and they get the queue, the transport and the now-playing line. The two phones talk directly and encrypted while they are in the same room: no account to create, no server in between, nothing to set up in the car.
